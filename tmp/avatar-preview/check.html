LEARN THE BASICS

How to play

Stack. Clear. Repeat.

Move and rotate falling pieces to fill a horizontal row of 10 blocks. A full row disappears and the blocks above fall down. The game ends when a new piece cannot enter the board.

Clear several rows at once for more points. The faint ghost piece previews your landing position.

Scoring & modes

One, two, three, or four lines earn 100, 300, 500, or 800 points × your current level. Hard drops earn 2 points per row traveled. Every 10 cleared lines raises the level.

Marathon ends when you top out. Sprint ends at 40 lines. Blitz ends after 2 minutes of active play. Practice has no automatic falling and no leaderboard ranking.
