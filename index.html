LEARN THE BASICS

☀HOW TO PLAY

1Objective

Stack, clear, and repeat. Fill a horizontal row of 10 blocks to clear it. The blocks above fall down.

Keep room at the top: the game ends when a new piece cannot enter. Enable the ghost piece in Settings to preview where your piece will land.

2Pieces

Seven different shapes, each made of four blocks.

I
L
J
O
S
Z
T

Use Hold to store a piece. Press Hold again to swap it with the current piece. The next five pieces appear beside the board.

3Controls (touch & mouse)

Mouse: Point over the board to move. Scroll the mouse wheel to rotate; left-click to hard drop.
Touch: Use gestures directly on the board.

←Swipe leftMove left
→Swipe rightMove right
↓Swipe downDrop one row
↑Swipe upHard drop
↻TapRotate clockwise
❚❚Hold 0.6sPause solo game

Tap a paused solo board to resume. Tap after game over to restart.

5Scoring

Clear several lines at once for more points.

1 line100 × Level
2 lines300 × Level
3 lines500 × Level
4 lines800 × Level

Soft drop: +1 per row. Hard drop: +2 per row. Combos, T-Spins, back-to-back clears, and perfect clears earn bonuses.

6Game modes

∞Marathon

Keep clearing lines. Every 10 lines raises the level and falling speed. Ends when you top out.

⚑40-line Sprint

Clear 40 lines as quickly as you can.

⚡Blitz

Score as much as possible in 2 minutes of active play.

◎Practice

No automatic falling by default. Enable gravity in Settings. Unranked.

⚔Online Versus

Same piece sequence. First to 40 lines wins, or survive when your opponent tops out.
